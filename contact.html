Practical

Good to know

Spring and autumn fill first — two to four weeks ahead is comfortable, and weekends go earlier. Short-notice trips are often possible midweek, so it is always worth a call.

Putah Creek is a tailwater, so it fishes year-round. Winter days are shorter and colder but the creek is quiet and the trout are still willing. Dress warmly and Richard will do the rest.

The Inn on First in Napa is a superb B&B and a long-standing friend of the business. Richard is happy to suggest places depending on whether you are coming for the fishing, the wine, or both.

Napa County Airport (APC) is closest for private aircraft. For commercial flights, Sacramento (SMF) and San Francisco (SFO) are both roughly an hour to an hour and a half away.
