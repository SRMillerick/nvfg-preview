The fine print, plainly

What's included

Included in every trip

  • Guided walk-and-wade access to Putah Creek, the lower Yuba or the American
  • Fly rods, reels, leaders, tippet and a working box of flies
  • On-water instruction in rigging, reading water and presenting a drift
  • Net, forceps and safe catch-and-release handling
  • Riverside lunch on full day trips
  • Photos of your fish, if you want them

Come prepared

What to bring

You'll want

  • A California sport fishing licence — buy it online before you travel
  • Polarised sunglasses and a brimmed hat
  • Layers, a waterproof shell and warm socks
  • Waders if you own them — otherwise rent a set for $25
  • A small daypack, water bottle and any snacks you like

Instruction

Lessons, casting & clinics

Richard is a patient, methodical teacher. He runs an on-the-river "Intro to Fly Fishing" class for complete beginners, and one- and two-lesson casting programs for anglers who want to fix a habit rather than learn from scratch.

He also gives presentations to clubs and groups on Putah Creek, the local tailwater fishery and catch-and-release practice.

Booking terms

Deposit & cancellation policy

How it works

  • Once your trip date is confirmed, a 50% deposit holds it. Cash, check or Venmo.
  • The deposit is non-refundable, but the date can be moved for weather or by request.
  • The balance is due on the day of the trip.
  • Gift certificates can be paid in full up front, with the date chosen later.
